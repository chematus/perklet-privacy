Can you earn Twitch Drops on mobile?

Yes. Twitch supports eligible Drops in its Android and iOS apps. Watch a participating live channel during an active campaign. Watching alone does not unlock subscription-only rewards. Perklet itself is Android-only.

Where to check Twitch Drops progress on your phone

Open Twitch Drops Inventory in your phone’s browser. Sign in to the account you use for watching to see progress, claimable rewards and claimed items. Twitch’s Drops icon or banner in chat also shows that channel’s progress.

How to claim Twitch Drops on mobile

When a reward is ready, select Claim in your inventory before its deadline. Claiming and in-game delivery are separate steps: follow the publisher’s account-linking or code-redemption instructions. Some iOS game rewards cannot be claimed inside Twitch’s iOS app.

For current rules, see Twitch’s official Drops guide.

Why is progress not counting, or a reward missing?

Check the campaign dates, participating channel and signed-in account. Watching extra streams does not speed up progress. If Twitch marks a reward claimed but it is missing in-game, check the linked game account and publisher’s delivery guidance.

Optional: track and auto-claim Drops with Perklet

You do not need Perklet to earn Twitch Drops. If you want an Android companion, Perklet organizes your games, monitors eligible watch-time rewards in the background and attempts to claim them when ready, without playing video on your phone.

  1. Connect Twitch. Open Settings → Twitch account and choose Connect Twitch. After signing in, check that Settings shows the Twitch account you intended to use.
  2. Choose a game. Search for it in Games, browse its campaigns and add it to your watching list. Check the reward requirements before starting; a publisher may ask you to link your game account.
  3. Turn on monitoring. Return to Home and switch Monitoring on. Check the monitoring card for its current state, and follow the app’s setup guidance if background access is missing.

Not sure which account to connect? See how Twitch and Google accounts differ.

Check progress and claimed rewards in Perklet

Check Home for the current reward, progress and estimated time. Only one reward advances at a time, so a different game’s progress may wait while that reward is being monitored.

When a reward is ready, Perklet attempts to claim it. To check what was claimed, open Rewards or choose View all next to Recent rewards on Home.

To compare Perklet with Twitch’s own record, use the inventory check above with the same Twitch account.

Change your monitoring priority

On Home, choose Manage next to Watched games, then drag a game by its reorder handle to move it up the list. On Free, move the game you want monitored to the very top.

An Inactive card is still saved. Tap the card away from its controls to see why it is inactive. If the first game has no eligible reward or live channel, Free waits rather than switching to an inactive game.

For the tier differences and what happens when Premium ends, see Free and Premium explained.

If Perklet monitoring stops

  • Check the campaign. It may have ended or have account, channel or purchase requirements. Not every reward is a watch-time Drop.
  • Check Monitoring and your connection. Monitoring must be on and your phone online. Background restrictions can interrupt Perklet; review the setup guidance in the app.
  • Check your accounts. In Settings, confirm that Twitch is connected to the account you intended. A game account may need a separate link on the publisher’s website.
  • Check the active game. On Free, only the first saved game is monitored. Reordering the list changes that choice.

If Twitch is temporarily unavailable, try a normal refresh later rather than clearing storage or reinstalling. If the problem persists, contact support with what you were trying to do. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.
